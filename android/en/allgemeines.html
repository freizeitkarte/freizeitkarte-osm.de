Underlying data
The Freizeitkarte maps "Summer 2024" are based on OpenStreetMap data from 2024/06/10. Thanks to all the mappers for their contribution to the OSM project.

Change log for release "Summer 2024":

  • Improvement of map data by the OSM project.
  • Improvement of contour lines:
    • LiDAR: SONNY's LiDAR DIGITAL TERRAIN MODELS (DTM) OF EUROPEAN COUNTRIES (sonny.4lima.de/)
    • SRTM-1: U.S. Geological Survey (usgs.gov/centers/eros)
    • ALOS-1: AW3D30 - JAXA - Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (earth.jaxa.jp/en/)
  • The labelling of the contour lines now always points uphill.
  • Improvement of the map visualisation.
  • Revision of the map cuts.
  • Revision of the web pages.

Next release
The release "Autumn 2024" is planned for end of September 2024.

General
The Freizeitkarte maps always consist of two parts:

  • the map data (*.map)
  • the map design (theme, design)

Installation
For security reasons, each map app must include the maps in its data content:

  • The map app offers the import (download) of a map as an internal function (e. g. OruxMap, Cruiser, c:geo).
  • Action: The user selects the corresponding function within the map app.
  • A link with control instructions is transferred from the Android browser to the map app (e. g. OruxMap, Locus Maps).
  • Action: The user activates the corresponding link in the browser of their Android device.

Download
The download of a Freizeitkarte map or a theme is possible:

direct to your personal computer (the map themes must also be loaded)
direct into the Android app Locus Map (the map themes are included in every map)
direct into the Android app OruxMaps (the map themes must also be loaded)

Hints

  • When downloading into Locus Map or OruxMaps a large amount of data will be loaded into your Android device.
  • You start the download to Locus Map or OruxMaps by choosing the appropriate link in your Android browser.
